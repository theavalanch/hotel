Frequently Inquired Information

What are your check-in and check-out policies?

+
Standard check-in begins at 3:00 PM and check-out is at 12:00 PM (noon). Early check-in or late departure can be arranged based on suite availability by requesting it during booking or contacting the concierge.

What is your reservation cancellation policy?

+
All direct web reservations may be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours prior to your check-in date. Cancellations can be completed instantly inside the "My Stays" portal using your reference number.

Do you offer private airport transportation?

+
Yes. We operate our private fleet of Rolls-Royce Ghost and Mercedes Maybach limousines providing airport express transfers. Rooftop helicopter transit to our certified helipad is also available upon inquiry.

Can I book a suite as a guest without creating an account?

+
Absolutely! Guest checkout is fully enabled. Simply input your contact details during reservation, and an instant booking confirmation reference code will be generated immediately.
